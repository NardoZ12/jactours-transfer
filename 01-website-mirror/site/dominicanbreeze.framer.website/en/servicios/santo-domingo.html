Colonial Zone Tour of Santo Domingo
Embark on a historical journey through the first city of the New World, declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Walk through colonial streets, experience history in every corner, and let yourself be captivated by the magic of Santo Domingo.

  • Los Tres Ojos National Park 🌊 – Mysterious caves with sacred underground lakes for the Taínos.

  • America's Primatial Cathedral ⛪ – The first cathedral on the continent, a Gothic and Renaissance gem.

  • National Pantheon 🇩🇴 – Mausoleum of national heroes in an 18th-century Jesuit temple.

  • Colón's Alcázar 🏰 – Renaissance palace of Diego Colón that revives the grandeur of the past.

✨ An essential experience for lovers of history, culture, and photography, in the heart of the first European city in America.
